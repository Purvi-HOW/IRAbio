The opportunity

Policy has turned CBG demand into an obligation.

India's city gas distributors must now blend compressed biogas into the CNG and PNG they sell. The mandate steps up every year to 2028–29, while installed supply remains a fraction of what it will require.

~108 / 1,094

Plants commissioned against active LoIs

SATAT targets 5,000 plants producing 15 million tonnes of CBG a year. As of July 2025, about 108 plants were commissioned against roughly 1,094 active letters of intent.

85%

Of average CNG retail price

From 1 June 2025 the SATAT procurement price was reset to 85% of the average CNG retail price, with transport support of ₹1.50–2.50 per kg beyond 50 km.

₹10 CR

Central Financial Assistance cap

MNRE provides ₹4 crore per 4.8 TPD of CBG capacity, up to ₹10 crore per project. CBG plants also qualify for priority sector lending.

750

New CBG projects targeted by 2028–29

The government expects the blending obligation to draw about ₹37,500 crore of investment into the sector.

How a plant works

From field to fuel, and back to the field.

Every tonne of grass yields two products. The gas goes to market; the digestate returns to the same farms as fertiliser, closing the nutrient loop that keeps the catchment productive.

01 · GROW

Harvest and ensile

Grass from the contracted catchment is chopped and stored as silage to buffer supply.

02 · DIGEST

Anaerobic digestion

High-load reactors convert feedstock and a liquid co-substrate into raw biogas.

03 · UPGRADE

Purify to fuel grade

CO₂, H₂S and moisture are removed to meet IS 16087:2016.

04 · DISPATCH

Compress and deliver

Gas is compressed to 250 bar and delivered in cascades to buyers.

05 · RETURN

Manure to the soil

Digestate becomes FOM and LFOM, enriched with chelated micronutrients.

Automation

Feedstock security

We grow our own feedstock.

Feedstock is the main operating risk in CBG. Plants that rely on crop residues depend on a short harvest window and volatile prices. Each IraBio plant is paired with its own Napier grass catchment under long-term contract.

Business model

Two products from every tonne.

Each plant earns from contracted gas sales and from organic manure, with further value from captured CO₂ and carbon credits. The base case rests on long-term offtake; direct retail is upside.

Impact

Cleaner fuel, healthier soil, steadier farm income.

Each plant replaces imported fossil gas with domestic renewable gas and returns organic carbon to farmland. Impact figures for the programme are reported in the data room with their methodology.

Energy security

Every tonne of CBG displaces imported LNG in city gas networks, supporting the national blending mandate and saving foreign exchange.

Soil health

Fermented organic manure restores soil carbon and microbial activity and reduces dependence on chemical fertilisers.

Rural livelihoods

Long-term contracts give farmers an assured market for an energy crop, and each plant creates skilled rural employment.
